Delete your Beacon account

Last updated October 4, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in Beacon, the pickup sports app for iOS and Android, what is deleted, and what is kept. You can also request deletion here if you no longer have the app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Beacon app and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you want to use Beacon again later, you will need to create a new account.

What is deleted

Everything described in our Privacy Policy that is linked to your account, including:

  • your email address, password, display name, date of birth, gender, city and Terms acceptance record;
  • your game and group memberships and check-ins;
  • games you host (they are cancelled and removed) and groups you host, including their chat history;
  • your messages in group chats; and
  • your blocked users list.

Beacon does not store your location, so there is no location history to delete.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: copies of deleted data in our backups are purged within 30 days.
  • Safety reports: reports you filed about other users are kept for up to 12 months in anonymised form (no longer linked to you), so we can identify repeat abuse and keep the community safe.

If you also joined the waitlist, tick the waitlist box in the form below and we will delete your waitlist entry too.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, submit this form. We will verify the request by contacting the email address on the account, then delete the account and confirm. Requests are completed within 30 days.
